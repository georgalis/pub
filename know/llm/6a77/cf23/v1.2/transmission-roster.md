# Transmission Configurations: A Reference Roster

### Thirty-six postures over the professional range, with gradients

Every entry here is computed from the transform rather than composed by
hand. That is the point of the roster: a grid whose cells were set
individually would record taste, and a grid whose cells are derived
tests whether the transform is complete. It was not, and the corrections
appear below before the grid that produced them.

Use the roster two ways. Where the reader matches an entry, take the
configuration. Where the reader falls between entries, take the two
nearest and read the direction of difference---which is what the grid
exists to supply and a genre list cannot.

## Configuration Templates

Two forms, given here rather than after the discussion because this is
what the roster is opened for. The first is what a writer states; the
second is the full calibration, for overriding a setting during revision.

```
/transmission
  fluency=0.60  stamina=0.50  appetite=0.40  scrutiny=0.15
  triage=full   obligation=0.90
  office=constructive  cadence=misura  priority=content  span=
```

```
posture     fluency=0.60  stamina=0.50  appetite=0.40  scrutiny=0.15
            triage=full   obligation=0.90
document    office=constructive  cadence=misura  priority=content  span=
load        spread=0.53   sparse=0.50   plain=0.42   grounded=0.65
land        steady=0.85   deference=0.35  single=0.50  placement=deferred
vantage     settled=0.50  linked=0.50   flat=0.85    bare=0.90
            iuxta=0.20    sequent=0.30  attribution=artifact
```

The values shown are the settled-professional default, which is roster
entry C2-S1-T1 computed rather than chosen. Any key left unstated resolves
to the value above and widens its tolerance, so an under-specified request
looks under-specified rather than average. Overwrite what should move.

The categorical options:

```
triage       full         reads it through
             scan         reads headings, descends selectively
             decide       reads the opening and decides
             sentence     meets one sentence in passing
office       constructive facts are inventory for what to build next
             judicial     facts are evidence about decisions already taken
cadence      staccato     short declaratives dominant
             misura       medium sentences, short ones as punctuation
             legato       long-breath sentences, each resolving in turn
priority     content      length floats, detail is preserved
             balanced     the shortfall is distributed
             extent       the span holds, content is cut
placement    early        stated in the opening, developed after
             deferred     arrives once the development has earned it
             coda         deferred, and the message restates the development
             frame        early, with a coda return
             sentence     the whole document is the message
attribution  agent        claims anchored to persons
             artifact     claims anchored to documents
             impersonal   claims anchored to conditions
```

Entries print in this order throughout: posture, then the document-side
declarations, then load, land, and vantage. Load comes first among the
instruments because its settings are what a reader's complaint most often
points at, land second because its settings are decided before drafting,
and vantage last because its costs are the silent ones and it is the
instrument that remains when the other two do not explain the reception.

## What Construction Corrected

Generating thirty-six configurations from five posture components
required the transform to be executable, and four settings turned out to
have no governing component. They had been defined without being
derivable, which is invisible while configurations are set by hand and
fatal the moment they are computed.

**`grounded` takes stamina as well as fluency**, and the pairing is not
decorative. Fluency decides whether the reader holds a compressed form;
stamina decides whether a reader who lacks it can infer it from context.
The two disagree routinely in the population the roster serves most---a
specialist at the end of a hard week holds every term and can assemble
no new one---and the disagreement rule resolves the setting high.

**`linked` takes stamina**, alongside `settled`. Both reduce
re-anchoring cost, and a reader who cannot carry a reorientation cannot
carry a cold sentence opening either.

**`single` takes triage**, directly: the less of a document a reader
consumes, the more the recurring vocabulary must reduce to the message.
A coupling rides on top---where `linked` sits at 0.70 or above, `single`
rises a further 0.15, since contiguous prose has surrendered its cuts
and recurrence is the only emphasis left.

**`cadence` is not posture-governed at all**, which took a wrong turn to
establish. Deriving it from `spread` generates the grid cleanly and
forbids a register that exists: long-breath sentences carrying
concentrated figures, which is dense argumentative prose and is correct
for the same specialist a reference card serves in `staccato`. Cadence
is a fact about what kind of document this is. The one enforced
constraint is the exclusion the load instrument already stated---`spread`
at 0.70 and above cannot be carried in short declaratives---and below
that, cadence is declared rather than derived.

**`steady` derives from headroom and scrutiny.** A depleted reader is
destabilized by valence movement, and an adversarial one reads calm
about serious matters as command of the situation. Both push high, and
the grid holds `steady` above 0.80 throughout---which is correct for
every reader who is obliged to be reading, and wrong for every reader
who is not.

The cadence correction generalizes past cadence. Four inputs come from
the writer rather than the reader: `cadence` from document kind, `steady`
from genuine emergency, `priority` wherever extent is fixed by a form or
a limit outside the reading, and `office` from the document's office. The
transform is reader-driven with exactly those four exceptions, and each
is a question about what the writer is making rather than about who will
receive it.

Two settings also needed conflict rules rather than single derivations.
**`flat`** takes the higher of its headroom-derived and scrutiny-derived
values, conceding 0.15 toward the scrutiny pull where they disagree,
which is the yield law applied to a known conflict rather than to
uncertainty. **`bare`** takes a headroom ceiling as well as its scrutiny
value, since pre-emptive defense is itself load and a depleted reader
cannot carry it however adversarial the setting.

### A sixth posture component

The grid holds `steady` high everywhere, and that exposed a fact the
five components could not express. Two readers may share fluency,
stamina, appetite, scrutiny, and triage and still want opposite
affective weather, because one is obliged to read and the other is not.
A professional who opens a memo and decides whether to descend, and a
casual reader who meets an explainer and decides whether to continue,
perform the same triage from entirely different positions: the first
owes the writer the reading, and the second owes nothing and departs
without complaint.

**obligation** --- how far the reader is obliged to read at all. 1.0 is
a reader whose attention is secured by role or relationship. 0.0 is a
reader present by choice, who will leave the moment the prose asks for
effort.

Obligation is a posture component and carries full standing in the
ontology. The grid nonetheless holds it high, which covers professional
correspondence entirely, and handles low obligation as an overlay rather
than as a fourth axis---voluntary readers cluster tightly at two triage
behaviors, and a full cross would double the roster to populate cells
that do not occur. The overlay is a presentation economy and should not
be read as ontological status. The overlay follows the grid.

### The office declaration

Version 1.1 adds a fourth writer-supplied input and the two settings it
floors. **office** declares what the document does with its facts:
`constructive`, where
the facts are inventory for what to build next and sequence is largely
arbitrary; `judicial`, where the facts are evidence about decisions
already taken and the question is not what is reasonable altogether but
what was reasonable at each point in time. **iuxta** sets how far each
claim arrives carrying the knowledge state it belonged to. **sequent**
sets how closely composition sequence tracks chronology.

Both are reader-derived within floors the declaration sets, and the
floors do not yield. `sequent` takes stamina through capacity, less a
declared discount of 0.20, since re-timing cost is real but smaller than
reorientation cost and topical order supplies its own economy. `iuxta`
takes scrutiny directly, an adversarial reader re-timing the material
whether or not invited.

The pair is generated together. Where `sequent` runs more than 0.35
above `iuxta`, the transform raises `iuxta` to close the gap; the
excluded region is the bare chronology---perfect order carrying no
epoch---which is the configuration that manufactures a false verdict
most efficiently, precisely because its ordering discipline is visible
and its epoch silence is not. Both constants are declared rather than
measured, and they are the framework's live tuning surface.

Like obligation, the judicial case is handled as an overlay. It follows
the grid.

## Reading an Entry

The identifier is headroom band, scrutiny level, triage behavior. Headroom
bands run C1 low, C2 mid, C3 high; scrutiny runs S1 settled, S2 guarded,
S3 adversarial; triage runs T1 full, T2 scan, T3 decide, T4 sentence.

Entries print in the order given in the templates above: posture, then
the document-side declarations, then load, land, and vantage, with the
categorical setting last within each instrument.

Each entry is declared `constructive` on the `office` line, which is the
default and the case the grid spans. The judicial declaration is an
overlay following the grid, and it replaces rather than adjusts the
fields it touches.

Every scalar carries a tolerance of plus or minus 0.10, widening to 0.20
wherever the components governing it disagree by more than 0.30. The
tolerance is the experimental range, not a margin of error: a first
attempt takes the center, a second takes the edge the first attempt's
reception implicates, and a third is synthesized from the difference.

The bullets beneath each entry are its **gradients**---the single-setting
moves that adjust it for the nearest posture deviations. An entry with
gradients is a local map; an entry without them is a lookup, and lookups
fail exactly when the reader is not on the list.

Headroom bands are composites. Where fluency, stamina, and appetite pull
apart, the grid gives the band and the gradients give the decomposition.

## The Office Overlay

Every entry above is declared `constructive`: its facts are inventory for
what to build next, and sequence is largely arbitrary. Declaring
`judicial`---the facts are evidence about decisions already taken, and the
question is not what is reasonable altogether but what was reasonable at
each point in time---moves at most four fields. Everything else holds,
which is why the judicial case is an overlay rather than a fourth axis.

Read the deltas as replacements rather than adjustments. `iuxta` and
`sequent` take floors from the declaration and do not yield to the reader;
where a cell already sits at or above a floor the floor is silent and the
field does not appear below. `placement` and `single` move only in
consequence: composition sequence has been handed to the chronology, so
arrangement is no longer available to carry emphasis and recurrence takes
the load it drops.

## Worked Derivations

The roster and the derivations are one artifact seen from two sides. A
derivation is the transform applied to one posture with its reasoning
shown; the grid is the set of derivations whose results were worth
caching. Three are worked here---the canonical case, a conflict, and an
off-grid posture---and the procedure they demonstrate generates the
rest.

### C2-S1-T1, the settled executive

*Posture.* Fluency 0.60: the reader knows the domain without living in
it. Stamina 0.50: attention secured, time scarce. Appetite 0.40: wants
the finding and holds the craftsmanship that produced it as someone
else's concern. Scrutiny 0.15: not evaluating the writer. Triage full:
reads it through or delegates it entirely.

*Capacity* is the lower of fluency and stamina, so 0.50, and the base
for the capacity-governed settings is 0.50. `settled`, `linked`, and
`sparse` take it directly.

*Appetite* at 0.40 sits marginally below neutral, nudging `spread` up to
0.53---the reader will not thank you for compression but is not injured
by its absence either. `plain` derives from fluency at 0.42, and
`grounded` from capacity plus the asymmetry allowance at 0.65, since a
term left unfounded degrades everything downstream while a term founded
unnecessarily costs one sentence.

*Scrutiny* at 0.15 puts `bare` at 0.90 and `attribution` at `artifact`.
The pre-emptive defense that an auditor requires reads to this reader as
a signal of trouble.

*Triage* full takes `placement` `deferred` and `priority` `content`.

*The result is unremarkable*, which is the finding. The canonical
professional document sits near the middle of nearly every setting, and
the roster's value is not here but at the cells where something pulls.

### C1-S3-T1, the depleted auditor

*Posture.* An adversarial reader by role, at the end of a long
engagement. Fluency 0.20 in this particular subject matter, stamina
0.15, appetite 0.25, scrutiny 0.85, triage full.

*The conflict is explicit.* Scrutiny at 0.85 wants `flat` at 0.30---three
perspectival strata per sentence, the provenance of every assertion
carried in its syntax. Capacity at 0.15 wants `flat` at 0.90, because a
reader in this state cannot hold three anchors open to the end of a
sentence.

*Yield resolves it.* The conflict rule takes the higher value, conceding
0.15 toward the scrutiny pull, and `flat` lands at 0.75. The
consequence is not that provenance is abandoned but that it moves: out
of syntax and into structure. `attribution` at `agent` names the source
in the sentence rather than nesting a stance about it, and an explicit
provenance apparatus---a source line, a citation, a table---carries what
layered framing would have carried.

*`bare` resolves the same way.* Scrutiny wants 0.15, the headroom ceiling
lifts it to 0.35. Every claim is still defended; the defense is short
and adjacent rather than woven through the assertion.

*This is the entry that justifies the grid.* Neither instinct alone
produces it. Configuring for the auditor produces prose the reader
cannot process; configuring for the depletion produces prose the reader
will not accept.

*Posture.* Fluency 0.25---this is not their field. Stamina 0.80, rested

## Using the Grid for Trial

The stated practice is to try one configuration, try a second, and
synthesize a third from what the two revealed. The grid supports it
directly, and the support is structural rather than incidental.

*The first attempt takes an entry's centers.* If the reception is
unsatisfactory, the reader's complaint identifies a setting through the
instruments' reverse diagnosis, and that setting's tolerance edge is
where the second attempt goes---not a fresh guess, but the same
configuration displaced 0.10 or 0.20 in the direction the complaint
indicates.

*The second attempt is often better because the first was correct for
the assessed posture and the assessment was slightly wrong.* The
difference between the two attempts measures the assessment error, and
the third configuration corrects the posture rather than the prose.
This is why the correction should be recorded against the posture
component and not against the document: a posture corrected fixes every
subsequent document for that reader.

*Where the two attempts bracket, interpolate.* Grid neighbors differ by
one band on one axis, so a target between them is a linear interpolation
with widened tolerance. Where they do not bracket---where both attempts
fail in the same direction---the posture was misassessed on a component
neither attempt varied, and the diagnosis returns to the transform.

## What the Grid Cannot Settle

Thirty-six cells span the professional range and do not exhaust it. The
grid holds obligation high and `office` constructive, treats headroom as a
composite, and samples each axis at three or four points. A reader whose components pull in
three directions at once is served by the derivation procedure and not
by any entry.

The grid also cannot validate itself. Its cells are computed from a
transform whose governance assignments are reasoned rather than
measured, and the four corrections recorded at the head of this document
were found by generating configurations, not by inspecting the rules.
Further corrections will arrive the same way: from a cell whose output
a practitioner recognizes as wrong, traced back to the assignment that
produced it.

## What Thirty-Six Cells Made Visible

The roster was commissioned to seed trial and error, and it did
something else first. Four settings had been defined precisely and left
underived, which no amount of hand-configuration would have exposed,
since a writer setting a value by judgment never notices that no rule
was available to set it. Computing the grid was the audit.

It also produced one wrong answer, and the wrong answer was worth more
than three of the right ones. Forced to derive `cadence` from something,
the transform derived it from `spread`, and the result forbade a
register that exists---which is how a model announces that it has been
asked for a determination the world does not supply. Three settings turn
out to take a document-side input, and the demand that everything derive
from the reader was itself the error.

The sixth posture component surfaced the same way, from a uniformity
rather than an error: `steady` held above 0.80 in all thirty-six cells,
and a setting that never varies across a spanning grid is either
constant in the world or governed by something the grid does not
contain. It was the second, and what the grid did not contain was the
difference between a reader who must read and a reader who may leave.
The instrument had been built by people writing to readers who had to
read them, and the assumption was so thoroughly satisfied that it had
never needed stating---which is the ordinary way a framework acquires a
blind spot, and the ordinary way a spanning grid finds one.

Both discoveries have the same shape, arriving from opposite
directions. A setting that would not vary had a cause outside the model;
a setting that had no cause was being forced to take one. Neither is
visible in a configuration set by hand, because judgment supplies the
missing rule silently and never reports having done so.
