# Transmission Load

The same content delivered through a short heavy conductor or a long
light one. This instrument sets power, not energy: how much arrives per
instant rather than how much arrives.

A reader's difficulty with prose frequently reflects the timing of
complexity rather than its quantity. Identical content, differently
distributed, arrives comfortably. Distribution is a position the prose
occupies, and a position moves in either direction---the operation that
unpacks a compressed contrast runs equally well in reverse, and
reference cards are built by running it backward on purpose.

## Reading the Settings

**0.0 is the compact, forceful pole; 1.0 is the gradual, low-pressure
pole.** Each setting is named for the quality maximal at 1.0. At 1.0 the
prose is spread, sparse, plain, and grounded.

A setting names a region to occupy, not a limit not to exceed. A low
`spread` calls for compression; it does not merely tolerate it.

Values read by position, not by scale: `20`, `2.0`, `0.2`, and `1/5`
place a setting identically.

Where a conflict forces a setting off center it yields high, toward the
light pole. The one exception: where appetite is confidently high,
`spread` yields low, since distribution offered to a reader who wanted
compression registers as time spent without consent.

## Template

```
load        spread=0.53  sparse=0.50  plain=0.42  grounded=0.65
```

```
cadence      staccato     short declaratives dominant
             misura       medium sentences, short ones as punctuation
             legato       long-breath sentences, each resolving in turn
```

Values are the settled-professional default. `cadence` is declared from
document kind rather than derived, and the full calibration is in the
`transmission` skill and in the roster.

## Invocation

`/transmission-load 0.2` sets all scalars to that band.
`/transmission-load spread=0.2 sparse=0.5 cadence=misura` overrides
individually. Bare invocation defaults to 0.6.

Ordinarily the configuration arrives from the `transmission` transform
rather than by direct setting.

## Settings

### spread --- contrast distribution

How far a contrast is distributed across the prose. The compact end is
the inventory of high-wattage figures: antithesis, negated contrast,
chiasmus, aphorism, dense coinage, triple construction---any formulation
whose entire force lands in a single clause, maximally precise per token
and maximally demanding per instant.

- **1.0** No peaks. Every contrast develops across a span of
  predications rather than detonating in one.
- **0.5** One high-wattage figure per paragraph, positioned at the close
  where accumulated context has covered its cost.
- **0.0** Compression is the register. Aphoristic construction
  throughout: expert reference cards, prompt artifacts, specifications
  written where no prior detail existed, research abstracts. The reader
  headroom is already there; use it.

**Relocation runs both ways.** Downward: identify the figure's two
poles, give each its own predication, let the contrast emerge from
adjacency, and spend the recovered emphasis on ordering so the decisive
pole lands last. Upward: collapse a developed contrast into a single
predication, drop the connective, let juxtaposition carry the logic.
Every term survives in both directions, and the precision is
undiminished.

Failure low, outside its context: serial detonation. Each formulation is
admirable and the sequence is exhausting. Comprehension survives; the
settled attentive state does not. Failure high: prose without profile,
nothing quotable, nothing carried forward.

### sparse --- concept arrival

How thinly new load-bearing concepts arrive. Each opens an account the
reader services until something closes it, and parallel open accounts
compound rather than queue.

- **1.0** One new concept per section. Intervening material develops,
  applies, and discharges it.
- **0.5** One per paragraph; supporting detail unrestricted.
- **0.0** Parallel arrival. Concepts land together and are disambiguated
  by their relations. Taxonomies, enumerations, reference matter,
  abstracts.

Failure low: the reader loses the thread mid-section though every
sentence is simple. Failure high: a reader who already holds the
material is walked through it, and gradualism offered to such a reader
lands as an imposition.

### plain --- lexical register

Word-level difficulty, which moves independently of how the content is
distributed.

- **1.0** Plain equivalents substituted wherever one exists at the same
  register. Any term retained is glossed at first use. Nominalizations
  converted where the actor is nameable.
- **0.5** Extended vocabulary where no same-register plain equivalent
  exists, glossed on first appearance.
- **0.0** Extended vocabulary preferred and travelling unglossed.
  Specialist register assumed rather than introduced.

Judge a word by its properties rather than by a list: syllable count,
length, whether the root is common in general use, and whether it
carries an abstracting suffix. A three-syllable term with a specialist
sense and a plain same-register synonym is the case this setting exists
to catch.

Failure low: the reader parses every sentence and understands none.
Failure high: a term of art replaced by a paraphrase that loses its
edge, which insults a fluent reader and misinforms a novice. Where no
same-register equivalent exists, this setting has nothing to offer and
`grounded` takes the work instead.

### grounded --- foundation before compression

Whether a compressed form has its foundation laid before the document
leans on it.

**What counts as a compressed form is decided by the reader, not by the
form.** A phrase-as-lemma the document coins, a named concept it
introduces, an acronym, a standard abbreviation, an established term of
art---all belong here whenever this reader does not already hold them.
Inherited jargon a reader lacks is functionally a coinage, and an
in-house abbreviation is not one to the house.

**This setting and `plain` are two answers to one problem.** The reader
lacks a term. `plain` substitutes: replace it with a same-register
equivalent and the difficulty goes away with the word. `grounded`
retains: keep the precise term and build the foundation that makes it
usable. The two trade against each other, and the case that matters is
the one where no equivalent exists at the same register---there `plain`
can do nothing without losing the distinction, and `grounded` carries
the whole burden. Low `plain` with high `grounded` is the configuration
for vital detail delivered to a reader who does not yet hold the
vocabulary: precise terms, fully taught.

The operation at high settings is consolidation. First occurrence
carries scaffolding sufficient for the meaning to arrive whole. A
proximate second occurrence, in the same passage or the next, signals
that the phrase repays retention. Subsequent occurrences rotate
grammatical position and modal frame, demonstrating range without
mechanical repetition. Only then does a back-reference retrieve the full
apparatus at compressed cost, and interval calibration follows---distant
enough to refresh, near enough to sustain recall.

- **1.0** Full consolidation with interval calibration. No compressed
  form invoked before its foundation is laid.
- **0.5** Enforced for forms invoked more than twice.
- **0.0** Coinage travels on first mention. Expert readers, dense
  reference artifacts, prompt material where the consuming model holds
  the definition already.

Fluency and stamina govern this jointly, and the pairing is the point.
Fluency decides whether the reader holds the form; stamina decides
whether a reader who lacks it can infer it from context. A rested reader
assembles a coinage from its first use and resents being taught it. A
depleted reader cannot, however fluent, and the two components routinely
disagree in exactly the population that matters---specialists at the end
of a hard week. Resolve high.

Failure low with a reader who lacks the vocabulary: compression
collapses into opaque shorthand, the back-reference points at a
foundation never laid, and everything downstream degrades quietly, the
reader having no way to identify where the trouble began. Failure high:
a reader who already holds the term is walked through its construction,
which costs a paragraph and a small insult.

Distinguish from `bare`, which is adjacent and separate. `bare` governs
whether a *claim* arrives defended. This governs whether a *term*
arrives usable.

### cadence --- sentence rhythm *(mode)*

The carrier wave across which distributed precision spreads.

- `staccato` --- short declaratives dominant, suspension free, high
  tolerance for parallel concept arrival. Recruitment, rosters,
  catalogs, alert register, reference cards. Re-concentrates load by
  shortening the span available to distribute it.
- `misura` --- alternating measure. Medium sentences with periodic short
  declaratives as punctuation. General-purpose.
- `legato` --- long-breath sentences, subordination-rich, each resolving
  fully before the next begins. The span-providing mode; pairs with high
  `spread`.

Cadence is declared from document kind, not derived from the reader. The
same reader takes different rhythms for a catalog and for an argument,
and long-breath sentences carrying concentrated figures are a real
register that no derivation should forbid.

One constraint is enforced regardless of document kind. `staccato` with
high `spread` cannot be satisfied: short sentences have no span to
distribute across, so `spread` at 0.70 and above excludes it. Resolve
toward `misura`, lower `spread`, or declare a local override so that
discursive sections and catalog sections run different rhythms.

## Composition Order

Distribution is architecture, not polish. Set cadence, position concept
arrival in the outline, ground every planned compression,
then audit drafted paragraphs against `spread`---relocating in whichever
direction the band requires.

Throttling concept arrival above a failed foundation only slows the
leak.

## Reverse Diagnosis

- "I had to read that twice" ---> `spread` too low. Understanding
  arrived faster than the reader could assemble it. Unpack the dense
  clause into successive predications, keeping every term.
- Prose reads flat, correct, unmemorable ---> `spread` too high, nothing
  concentrated enough to retain.
- "I lost the thread," said of individually simple sentences --->
  `sparse` too low, obligations accumulating faster than they discharge.
- Every sentence parses and nothing is understood ---> `plain` too low.
- "You used that word earlier---remind me?" ---> `grounded` incomplete,
  or the interval longer than retention supports.
- Rhythm feels mechanical ---> `cadence` fighting the `spread` band.

Each corrects one document. The posture component behind it corrects
every document for that reader, and that mapping belongs to the
`transmission` skill.

## Interactions

Where `spread` runs low throughout, density becomes the baseline rather
than the departure, and emphasis is no longer available through
contrast---`transmission-land` must work through placement and
recurrence instead. `sparse` and `grounded` are one operation: slowing
the arrival of concepts repays only where the concepts that arrive are
properly founded.

`iuxta` and `sequent` in `transmission-vantage` arrive on this
instrument's budget rather than on their own. Epoch marking and announced
ordering are content, not framing, so raising either adds tokens at fixed
compression, and under a fixed `priority` the difference is paid out of
`spread` and `plain`. Where that payment drops compression below the
reader's band, report the conflict instead of resolving it: extent and
distribution are both the writer's to trade, and neither is the
transform's to settle. A judicial document written to a low-headroom reader
is the case that produces it.

A maximum-density style is an adjacent, self-standing instrument. Low
`spread` with low `grounded` approximates its delivery profile but
not its generative inventory; these settings position prose, they do not
stock a lexicon. Invoke it directly when that inventory is wanted.
