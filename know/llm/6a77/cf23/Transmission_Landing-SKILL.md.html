Transmission Landing

Positions where a document spends its scarce resource: the reader's marked attention. Salience is purchased with uniformity, and the companion skills mint whatever currency this one spends. A document emphatic everywhere emphasizes nothing. A document uniform anywhere, at any pressure, with one placed event, emphasizes exactly that event.

The imperative need not be a directive. It is whatever the reader should carry away---an ask, a finding, a warning, a single idea.

Dial Semantics

Dials define a target band, not a ceiling.

Direction is uniform across the transmission skills: 0.0 is the compact, mobile, forceful pole; 1.0 is the steady, gradual, low-pressure pole.

Values are read by position, not by scale. 35, 3.5, 0.35, and 7/20 all set the same dial to the same place. Percent or decimal, as convenient.

Invocation

/transmission-landing 0.35 sets all scalar dials to that band. /transmission-landing placement=deferred sanction=0.35 affect=0.35 overrides individually. Bare invocation defaults to 0.5.

Qualities

placement --- imperative placement (discrete)

Position of the payload within the architecture. Selection follows the reader's triage behavior.

  • early --- stated in the opening, developed after. Suited to readers who triage by first paragraph and to documents whose body is reference matter. Cost: the imperative arrives before context has covered its weight, so it usually requires restatement.
  • deferred --- arrives after the development that makes it self-evident; the reader reaches the ask already agreeing. Maximum weight per statement. Cost: triage readers may never reach it.
  • coda --- deferred, plus enactment. The imperative restates the document's development in the act of asking. Highest craft cost, highest landing weight.
  • frame --- early statement with coda return. Spends double recurrence deliberately; correct for long documents where a single placement decays across the span, and for mixed audiences whose triage behavior is unknown.
  • sentence --- the entire document is the imperative. No development precedes it and none follows. Social posts, alerts, subject lines, reference-card headers. Placement collapses into composition.

Known full readers: deferred or coda. Unknown or triage-prone: frame. One sentence of available attention: sentence.

sanction --- sanction register

Authority posture of the imperative. Named positions along a scalar.

  • 0.0 mandate --- obligation stated as command. Compliance contexts where authority is the writer's to invoke.
  • 0.3 directive --- obligation stated as fact about the situation rather than command at the reader. Depersonalized mandate. Firm without confrontation; frequently the executive optimum.
  • 0.5 recommendation --- the imperative carried by evidence weight, the reader's authority to decide explicitly preserved.
  • 0.7 option-framing --- structured alternatives whose ordering does the persuading.
  • 1.0 invitation --- maximum reader autonomy, minimum delivered force. Upward influence without standing; ideas intended to mature as the reader's own.

Register and placement multiply. A mandate placed early reads as command; the same mandate deferred reads as conclusion. Soft registers tolerate early placement; forceful asks want deferral.

Failure signature: register above the writer's actual standing reads as overreach and converts a receptive reader into an evaluating one.

recurrence --- recurrence budget

How strictly repetition is reserved for the imperative's vocabulary.

Recurrence is the document's highlighting system---whatever recurs, the reader retains. Unbudgeted, a document highlights by accident: some subsidiary phrase repeats and departs carrying the emphasis the imperative needed. Budgeting awards the leitmotif position rather than drifting into it. Mechanism belongs to repetition-methodology; allocation belongs here.

  • 0.0 Unmanaged. Natural repetition stands.
  • 0.5 Imperative vocabulary receives deliberate recurrence; competing recurrence above three instances rotates to synonyms.
  • 1.0 Single-leitmotif discipline. Only imperative vocabulary recurs; other repetition is actively varied away. The document has exactly one memorable phrase and it is the payload.

affect --- affect stability

Ceiling on valence movement across the document.

Alarm, urgency spikes, and tonal whiplash convert a settled reader into a vigilant one, and vigilant readers evaluate defensively rather than receive. But valence movement is also the recruitment instrument for attention not yet won.

  • 0.0 Valence unrestricted. Dramatic register; genuine emergency, recruitment, popular writing that must earn its reader's presence.
  • 0.5 Gradual movement. Urgency may build across sections, never spike within one. No fear-and-reassurance oscillation.
  • 1.0 Flat. Gravity carried entirely by content and placement, never by tonal signaling. A document calm about serious things signals command of the situation.

Composition Order

Decided before drafting, not after: placement from the triage model, sanction from actual standing, recurrence awarded to the payload vocabulary. Then draft under companion-skill discipline, then audit for competing recurrence and unplanned valence movement.

Verification: name the phrase the reader repeats afterward. If it is not the payload, the budget failed.

Reverse Diagnosis

  • Readers report the document as informative but recall no action ---> placement mismatched to triage behavior; likely deferred where frame was needed.
  • The ask is resented rather than considered ---> sanction below standing.
  • The ask is ignored as optional ---> sanction above the situation's actual force.
  • Readers quote a subsidiary phrase back ---> recurrence budget lost to an accidental leitmotif.
  • Readers act, but anxiously ---> affect too low; alarm was contagious.
  • Readers do not arrive at all ---> affect too high for an audience whose attention had to be won first.

Cross-Skill

  • transmission-vantage --- supplies the smooth medium whose uniformity gives placement its contrast, and the cut that coincides with the imperative.
  • transmission-load --- supplies the pressure field. Where peak runs low, the imperative holds the single sanctioned exception; where peak runs high, contrast is unavailable and landing works through placement and recurrence instead.
  • reception --- overlaps partially with sanction. When both are active, the lower effective force governs.
  • session-retention --- orthogonal. That skill governs artifact self-containment, a structural obligation; it composes freely here and substitutes for nothing.
